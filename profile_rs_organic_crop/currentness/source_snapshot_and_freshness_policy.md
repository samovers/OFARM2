# Serbia Source Snapshot And Freshness Policy

Status: profile currentness policy for

not OFARM Core law.

Release posture:
`RS_ORGANIC_CROP_FARM_REG_CERT_IMPLEMENTATION_CANDIDATE_PRODUCTION_HOLD`

## Currentness Rules

- Live portals are current-state surfaces only unless snapshotted as
  transaction-time evidence.
- eRPG public lookup is corroborative, not historical proof.
- eAgrar, eSanduce, and UAP official artefacts are preferred for
  transaction-time farm registration proof.
- Annual Ministry authorised-control-body lists are year-versioned authority
  artefacts.
- ATS register evidence must be date-specific, scope-specific, and snapshotted.
- Suspended or withdrawn certificate checks must preserve the actual artefact
  consulted.
- Private farm or certifier artefacts may support a claim, but cannot alone
  prove regulated public authority status.
- Advisory sources never promote Compliance Twin state.

## Freshness Requirements

| Claim type | Freshness anchor | Refusal condition |
|---|---|---|
| Farm registration status | Transaction-date or claim-year official UAP/eRPG/eAgrar/eSanduce artefact | Only live lookup, missing status date, unreadable active/passive state, or missing renewal evidence |
| Organic certificate status | Certificate validity covering transaction date plus Ministry, ATS, and adverse-status snapshots for the same relevant period | Missing validity, unmatched subject/scope, missing dual-gate authority evidence, or missing adverse-status check |
| Certifier authority reference | Ministry annual list and ATS accreditation snapshot aligned to the certifier and date/scope | Either side missing, expired, suspended, withdrawn, wrong scope, or live-only |
| Review event | Challenged act plus official review artefact and known review state | Finality claimed while review window/status is unresolved |

No projection, cache, public lookup, or private website may become a hidden truth
store for high-consequence promotion.

## Legal-Source Posture

The 2025 Serbian Organic Production Law is treated as the profile research basis
for this narrow slice. The research summary indicates that the law applies from
1 June 2026 and pins the Ministry-led organic-subject register and certificate
concept closely enough for an implementation-candidate profile.

Production closure remains blocked until official packeted source artefacts are
committed or referenced by a controlled source packet. This profile therefore
uses the release posture
`RS_ORGANIC_CROP_FARM_REG_CERT_IMPLEMENTATION_CANDIDATE_PRODUCTION_HOLD`.

Do not paste or treat copyrighted legal text as profile content. This package
records article-level implications and profile evidence rules only.

## Minimum Certificate Field Floor

Until the post-2025 minister-prescribed certificate form is packeted, this
profile uses only the minimum statutory certificate field floor described by the
research summary:

- operator or organic subject identity;
- product category or certificate scope;
- certificate validity period;
- issuing control body identity where present;
- electronic certificate artefact where available.

Older by-law forms, legacy certificate formats, or transitional practice may
support review, but they must not be silently treated as post-2025 production
law. A certificate status assertion that depends on the unpinned form must refuse
or stay in review.

## Profile Boundary

This law posture supports only candidate evidence organization and refusal
design. It does not make a production-grade Serbia profile, certify an operator,
integrate a live register, or resolve the still-missing source packet artefacts.

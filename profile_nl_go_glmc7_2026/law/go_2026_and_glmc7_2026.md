## GO 2026 Filing Basis

The legal anchor for the 2026 `Gecombineerde Opgave` is Stcrt 2026, 3657,
`Regeling Landbouwtelling en Gecombineerde opgave 2026`.

For this slice:

- the GO is the 2026 annual filing instrument;
- the filing window is 1 March 2026 through 18 May 2026;
- relevant parcel and crop facts use the 15 May 2026 `peildatum`;
- filing is electronic through Mijn RVO;
- filing by an authorised representative requires an explicit authority route,
  such as the recorded RVO/eHerkenning authorisation or `machtiging`.

The GO filing proof is an evidence anchor. It does not by itself prove GLMC 7
compliance.

## GLMC 7 Amendment-Chain Basis

The GLMC 7 legal rule is reconstructed through the official amendment chain for
`Uitvoeringsregeling GLB 2023`, including Stcrt 2026, 1855. Under this release
posture, that amendment-chain reconstruction is accepted as the legal source
standard for the slice.

## Article 32 Organic / In-Conversion Route

Stcrt 2026, 1855 moves the organic and in-conversion route into Article 32. For
this profile, parcels certified under Regulation (EU) 2018/848 or in conversion
are treated through the Article 32 deemed-compliance route for GLMC 7 when valid
Skal or equivalent evidence is attached to the parcel and claim-year filing.

An organic or in-conversion assertion without proof does not promote.

## GLMC 7 Rule Elements

For 2026 Dutch arable land (`bouwland`), the GLMC 7 crop rotation slice evaluates
three rule elements:

1. Annual one-third rotation: each year at least one third of the total 2026
   `bouwland` must have a different main crop code than the prior year, or a
   qualifying follow-on crop route where the source supports it.
2. Once-in-four-years parcel rule: each parcel must have a different main crop
   at least once in the four-year window.
3. Sand/loess rustgewas rule: for parcels on sand or loess, a qualifying
   `rustgewas` must occur in the 1 January 2023 through 1 January 2027 window.

The 2026 RVO `Gewascodelijst` is the crop-code and crop-classification reference
for this slice, including GLMC 7 and `rustgewas` classifications. It is official
reference data for implementation and filing use, not primary law.

## Simplifications And Exclusions

RVO 2026 conditionality guidance describes an under-10-hectare simplification for
controls and sanctions. This profile treats that as control/sanction
simplification only. It is not encoded as removal of the underlying legal duty.

The 30-hectare GLMC 7 carve-out is not legally supported in the sources found and
is not encoded. Assertions relying on that carve-out must refuse or enter manual
legal hold.

## Review And Appeal Route

The appeal path preserved by this profile is:

`RVO decision -> bezwaar at RVO -> beslissing op bezwaar -> beroep at CBb`

An adverse RVO decision must not be treated as final and irreversible while a
timely `bezwaar` or `beroep` route is evidenced and unresolved.
